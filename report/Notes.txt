## Key findings

1 After data cleaning and formatting: 242,033 closed records and 40,802 open records
2 Inspection of the service requests still open at the end of 2024
2.1 Visual inspection of the ticket age distribution suggests peaks at 60 and 170 days 
(@fig-open-tickets-distribution). Because it is a visual observation it could be an artefact of data binning 
and it is first confirmed by changing binning (@fig-robust-binning). To completely confirm 
the existence of the two peaks a robust nonparametric test (Hartigan's Dip Test) is utilized
and yields an effect size of 0.02 and a p-value indistiguishible from 0.
2.2 The origin of the two peaks is then resolved by cross-referencing open requests and department.
The transportation department shows a surge of unclosed requests starting in June (170 days) followed
by a second one in Ocotber (60 days)(@@fig-open-tickets-by-month). These two surges correspond 
respectively to the beginning of the Sumner Tunnel Restoration Project and the temporary closure 
of the MBTA orange line.
